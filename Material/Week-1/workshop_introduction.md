# Who This Workshop Meant For
These workshops are intended for anyone who's:
- Struggling to complete game projects.
- New to game development (and game architecture).
- Someone with experience in another engine who wants to learn Godot.
- Just interested in learning composition-based game architecture!


# What Will Be Covered
The goal of these workshops is to teach important gamedev concepts (using GDScript with Godot) that basically all gamedev projects require. It'll primarily be focused on addressing the areas that usually hold back beginner game devs from being able to complete projects, especially for game jams. 

During this workshop, you'll be working a basic platformer and learning how to apply the theory I'll be teaching to this game. By the end, you should be able to finish the platformer and feel more confident to apply those concepts to your future projects :)

The specific subjects that'll be covered:
- Essential development skills (using documentation, debugging, breaking down problems, etc)
- Good practices that all projects should use for more efficient programming.
- How to use suitable composition-based architectures within your projects, to reduce friction of feature implementation. This will be the make focus and the bulk of material!


# In-Person Sessions
The in-person sessions will just be for teaching all the theory in more detail and in a more interactive way that may make the complex concepts easier to understand. It will still be based on the material within this repository and I'll be (hopefully) updating it right before each workshop with new material. You'll also have opportunities to complete exercises during the sessions and ask for any help if you're unsure about anything. You'll still need to do plenty of work outside the workshops though since we obviously don't have enough time for you to finish a whole game during them. The aim is that after the workshops you'll be able to easily apply the theory to your platformer. If you're unable to attend any session, you should be able to follow along using this repository!
